# 03. From volume complaints to delivery above contract

**Where.** Chainlabs, Regional Operations Team Lead, London. January 2026 to now.

## Context

I own customer success and client delivery for the Regional Exchanges vertical of a blockchain attribution-data business: three client delivery leads and a contributor network of 43 across 25+ countries. I am the named account lead for one client and delivery owner across three more, and I write every client progress report myself.

## The problem

Before I joined, clients had complained about volume. Each client contract sets a monthly minimum, and the vertical had been missing the mark often enough that the complaints reached the business.

## What I did

**The decision.** Make the contract floor visible to everyone delivering against it, then manage to the window rather than to the month end.

- Set an SLA per client that escalates at 66% and 85% of the window, and again at breach.
- Put a weekly delivery meeting and 1:1s with the three leads in place.
- Ran the hiring pipeline across 25+ countries and wrote the screening assessment researchers are hired against.
- Moved parts of the network from hourly to per-unit pricing, with cost-versus-output tracking so the margin is visible.
- Built the payout pipeline and the helper chat bot the network runs on, with the engineering team.

## Result

- August 2026 closed at about 275 entities against a contract floor of 200, with over 1,200 traces: 35 to 50% more than the contract requires, month after month.
- Year to date: 1,072 traces and 255 entities for one client, 541 entities under management for another, and about 13 days average turnaround.

## Also in this role

- Build, deploy and maintain the vertical's production web applications myself, and set up the Git, code review and CI/CD workflow the team ships through.
- The three consoles that took the vertical off Google Sheets: [case 04](04-three-consoles-off-google-sheets.md).
- The AI tooling behind the delivery: an LLM gateway with a cost ledger and kill switch, hybrid retrieval, and an eval gate before anything ships. Written up at [mally-cv.vercel.app/ai](https://mally-cv.vercel.app/ai).
