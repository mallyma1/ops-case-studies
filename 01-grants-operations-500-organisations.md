# 01. Grants operations for 500+ organisations

**Where.** Starknet Foundation, Operations Manager, London. May 2024 to December 2025.

## Context

The Starknet Foundation is a major Layer-2 blockchain foundation. I joined at its launch, specifically to build the grants programmes, and ran core operations across grants, compliance, treasury and ecosystem.

Grants went to external organisations ranging from solo developers to established companies, each with different technical maturity and support needs. I owned the full lifecycle for every one of them: technical and commercial review, contracting, milestone tracking, and payment on delivery.

## The problem

The same four steps (review, contract, milestones, payment) had to run for more than 500 organisations over the role, and no two were at the same level of technical maturity.

The Foundation had no AML/KYB framework of its own. (AML/KYB means anti-money-laundering and know-your-business checks on the organisations being paid.) Compliance, legal and finance requirements also had to line up across three jurisdictions: the US, the UK and the EU.

## What I did

**The decision.** Build the compliance checks into onboarding and automate them, so onboarding could scale and stay compliant.

- Designed the Foundation's first AML/KYB framework and integrated Sumsub automation into onboarding.
- Aligned compliance, legal and finance requirements across the US, UK and EU, working with specialists. I owned the operational and framework side of compliance and brought legal in for sign-off. I am not a lawyer.
- Reworked the onboarding and reporting process in Notion and Airtable.
- Built grant KPIs and OKRs, so delivery could be tracked and reported to leadership.
- Applied LLM-based quality checks to contract summaries and compliance documentation.

## Result

- Onboarding and reporting cycle times cut by 33%.
- 500+ organisations onboarded and delivered for.
- The Foundation's first AML/KYB framework, with Sumsub automation in onboarding.
- Compliance, legal and finance requirements aligned across the US, UK and EU.
- The grants programmes, seed grants among them, are still running.

## Also in this role

- Supported management of $100M+ in assets across multisig and Starknet-native wallets, with reconciliation and tracking across multi-currency flows.
- Managed $300K in donations and coordinated exchange listings, OTC flows and custodian partnerships.
- Supported hundreds of teams on funding, reporting, compliance and operational readiness, and coordinated hackathons, developer programmes, meetups and ambassador initiatives.

## Evidence and limits

- **Public.** The grants programmes are still running.
- **Belongs to the Foundation.** The Notion and Airtable workspaces, the KPIs and OKRs, and the AML/KYB framework itself. None of it is reproduced here.
- **Not recorded.** The baseline and the measurement period behind the 33% figure.
- **Not measured.** The effect of the KPIs and OKRs on delivery accuracy, and the effect of the LLM checks on error rates.
- **Left out on purpose.** Recipient names, grant amounts and internal documents.
