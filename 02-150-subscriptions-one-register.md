# 02. 150+ subscriptions into one register

**Where.** Parity Technologies (Polkadot), London. Operations Coordinator, July 2022 to mid-2023, then Operations Project Manager, mid-2023 to March 2024.

## Context

I joined as Parity's first operations hire in the UK.

To learn how the company actually worked, I went department by department. I sat with engineering, marketing, ecosystem development and devrel to understand the systems, processes and tools each one already used.

## The problem

Each team ran on its own set of tools, and across them the company was paying for more than 150 subscriptions, €3M in annual billings. They were not held in one place, so there was no single register for tracking what they cost or managing the vendors behind them.

## What I did

**The decision.** One register, in Asana, with Asana as the backbone connected to the tools each team already used.

- Centralised all 150+ subscriptions into Asana for cost tracking and vendor management.
- Brought in the integrations that made the departments' tools work together, with Asana as the backbone alongside GitHub Projects, Notion and Airtable.
- Owned the Notion estate alongside the register.
- Promoted about a year in, once the London office onboarding was set up. As Asana Champion I drove adoption, trained teams, audited their workspaces day to day and tightened how each one ran its projects.
- Worked with the General Counsel, Chief Security Officer and COO to centralise procurement and to run the Bug Bounty Program, with payouts in fiat and in DOT (the Polkadot token), approvals, reconciliations and audit-ready documentation.

## Result

- 150+ subscriptions, €3M in annual billings, in one register for cost tracking and vendor management.
- Asana as the backbone, connected to GitHub Projects, Notion and Airtable.
- As the first UK operations hire, set up the London office (procurement, vendor selection, security and documentation), then supported a second European office opening.
- Savings: not measured.

## Also in this role

- Ran onboarding for the entire UK division with legal, security, HR and finance, and rebuilt the onboarding and offboarding SOPs across all four.
- Supported the Polkadot Developer Heroes Program, onboarding 100+ developers.
- DevOps and security operations with the security team: access and devices for joiners, movers and leavers, security review of tools and vendors before procurement, and office and device security with its policies and documentation.

## Evidence and limits

- **Belongs to Parity.** The Asana and Notion workspaces and the register itself. Nothing from them is reproduced here.
- **Figure.** €3M is the annual billings across the register as recorded at the time. It is not broken down by year here.
- **Dates.** The promotion came about a year in; the month is not in my records, so the case says mid-2023.
- **Not measured.** Savings from cancelled or consolidated subscriptions, and any change in the time spent tracking them.
- **Left out on purpose.** Vendor names and contract figures.
